How AgroCycle Works

Connecting farmers with buyers to reduce crop waste and create value from agricultural residues

For Sellers (I HAVE crop waste)

1

Register & Create Your Profile

Sign up for free as a Farmer/Buyer. Add your location details so buyers near you can find your listings easily.

2

Post a Listing

Click "Post My Crop Waste" from your dashboard. Fill in the product name, category (paddy straw, wheat straw, etc.), quantity, price per unit, and add photos. Choose "I HAVE" as listing type.

3

Receive & Approve Orders

Buyers will send order requests. You'll get a notification. Review the request and approve or reject it. You control who buys your material.

4

Arrange Delivery & Get Paid

Once approved, our system finds a delivery person. They pick up the material and deliver it. Complete the transaction and earn income from waste that used to be burned!

For Buyers (I NEED crop waste)

1

Browse the Marketplace

Search by crop type, location, price range, or quantity. Filter for exactly what your farm or business needs.

2

Request Material

Click on a listing, enter the quantity you need and your delivery address. Submit the order request to the seller.

3

Seller Approves, Delivery is Arranged

If the seller approves, a delivery person is automatically matched and assigned. You'll receive an OTP for delivery confirmation.

4

Receive & Confirm

Once delivered, share the OTP with the delivery person, then confirm receipt on the platform. Leave a review for the seller!

👨‍🌾
