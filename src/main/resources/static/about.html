About AgroCycle

Turning agricultural waste into opportunity, one transaction at a time

Our Mission

AgroCycle was built to solve one of agriculture's most persistent problems: crop residue burning. Every year, millions of tonnes of paddy straw, wheat straw, sugarcane bagasse and other agricultural waste are burned in open fields, contributing to air pollution and destroying soil microorganisms.

We connect farmers who have this "waste" with businesses and individuals who see it as a valuable raw material — for composting, mushroom cultivation, animal feed, biomass energy, and more. By creating a transparent marketplace, we help farmers earn additional income while reducing environmental harm.

Technology Stack

AgroCycle is built with Java Spring Boot, MySQL, and vanilla JavaScript. No tracking, no ads, no third-party surveillance. Your farm data stays on our secure servers and is never sold.

The platform features smart delivery matching using geographical algorithms, real-time in-app notifications, full order lifecycle management, and secure session-based authentication.
