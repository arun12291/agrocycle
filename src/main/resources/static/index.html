How AgroCycle Works

📝

1. Register

Create a free account as a farmer, buyer, or both. One account for buying and selling.

📦

2. Post or Find

Post what you have or browse what you need. Filter by category, location and price.

🤝

3. Connect & Agree

Buyer requests a quantity. Seller reviews and accepts or declines. You're notified instantly.

🚚

4. Automatic Delivery

AgroCycle automatically finds a nearby delivery person. Track status from pickup to delivery.

✅

5. Confirm & Review

Confirm delivery with a secure OTP. Leave a review to help the community.
